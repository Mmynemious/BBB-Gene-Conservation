# Reading guide

A working aid, not site content. Keep it beside the paper.

## Marking key

Matched to the colours already in use, so the scan itself carries the structure.

| Colour | Mark this |
|---|---|
| **Yellow** | The claim. What the paper says it found. |
| **Green** | Method and model. What was done, to what, under what conditions. |
| **Blue** | Numbers worth quoting. Ratios, fold changes, N, p values. |
| **Pink** | Anything that surprised you, contradicted something, or looked weak. |
| **Margin note** | Your own words. Questions, connections, objections. |

Pink and margin notes are the ones that matter most. Yellow, green and blue can be
reconstructed from the PDF; pink and margin cannot.

## While reading, capture the six fields

Write these in the margin of page 1 as you find them, so they are in one place afterwards.

1. **Year**
2. **Models** — which species, and is the cross-species claim made on the same tissue
3. **Brain region** — stated, not stated, or not matched between species
4. **Measures** — DNA, RNA, protein, or function
5. **Method** — and for sequencing, bulk or single cell, sorted or whole tissue
6. **Cross-species stats** — orthologues, tests, thresholds, correction

## Then talk through it in this order

This maps onto the entry sections, so nothing has to be reordered afterwards.

1. **The six fields**, in order. Short answers.
2. **What the paper is really doing.** Not what it reports, what it is *for*. Is it making a
   biological claim, defending a method, or building a resource? This is the part that cannot
   be read off the PDF, and it is the most valuable thing you say.
3. **What surprised you or did not add up.** Your pink marks.
4. **What you did not understand.** Terms go to the glossary; sections skimmed get noted so
   they can be revisited.
5. **What it means for this project.** Does it support, undercut, or supply something the
   pipeline lacks. Say it even if the connection is loose, and especially if it is awkward.

## Do not bother dictating

- The order of the methods sections
- What each figure shows
- Numbers, unless you want to comment on one

All of that comes off the PDF. Effort spent there is effort not spent on 2 to 5 above.
