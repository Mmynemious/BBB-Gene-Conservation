My reading

I had a look again at the papers I have. Miao and the others from this year is probably the best paper we have on transcriptomes of blood brain barrier capillary endothelial cells, and on how they did the analysis. But for us specifically, if we want to concentrate on endothelial cells, apparently there is no identical pipeline we could use.

There is something by Brendo which identifies genes selectively enriched in brain microvascular endothelial cells versus endothelial cells from other tissues, using bulk RNA sequencing. The important thing here is that it shows how it works with multiple datasets and quality control.

Now I am a bit confused as to how to approach this currently, assuming we want to use endothelial cells. Miao et al. showed that even if they looked at a very broad gene selection, in a brain part I do not remember, there was only a small part which overlapped in gene expression between the species, and most of them were transporter proteins.

There is one graph that I want to understand in order to figure out how we want to continue.

Checks against the paper

  • Is there a pipeline identical to this project? Not here. Brendo compares brain endothelium against other tissues’ endothelium within human only, which matches this project’s enrichment step rather than its cross-species step.
  • Which brain part did Miao look at, and how much overlapped? Mouse was whole brain vasculature; human was post-mortem brain and tissue next to gliomas. The overlap was broad (correlation 0.68). The genes that differed between species, 169 and 386, were where the SLC transporters stood out.
